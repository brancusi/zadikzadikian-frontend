Opens every major section of a proposal: 2px black rule, letterspaced uppercase label with right-aligned index, then a large Caslon title.

```jsx
<SectionHeading index="02" label="Future work" title="The Studio, continued" />
```

`size`: `colossal` (cover-scale, clamps 72–128px), `display` (64px, default), `headline` (40px).
