Academic skill, archetype, fragment, and erotic form. Even the most serial stacks remain calibrated to bodily movement, reach, labor, and architectural passage.

Architecture as medium; color and gold as environmental conditions. Pale yellow unified 112 Greene Street in 1973 while open doors and windows let air enter the work; the gilded rooms followed — acts of equalization, not decoration.

Brick, ingot, serial order, air, interval, and portable construction. The longitudinal backbone of the practice, from the lost Tehran bricks of 1978 to Path to Nine and Made in USA.

Gravity, action, casting, contingency, and recognizable form at the edge of abstraction. In Foreigners, wet clay is lifted and thrown so that faces emerge through impact — the individual appears through the rule.

Color, touch, movement, and work that remains in formation. The 2026 Armenian Pavilion displaces gold with pigment-infused movable blocks produced and rearranged in public — from a common surface to a common procedure.
