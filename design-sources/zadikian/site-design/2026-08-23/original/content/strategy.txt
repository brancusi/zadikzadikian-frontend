[Heading1] 1. Strategic objective
THE JOB OF THE SITE  Make the case for Zadikian as a historically consequential participant in postwar American sculpture by allowing the work, chronology, and archival evidence to accumulate. The site should feel like the beginning of serious scholarship, not a campaign for recognition.
The current site contains a broad image archive and several valuable first-person texts, but its flat collection list does not explain the career. The redesign must create a retrospective path through the work while preserving a complete index for scholars, curators, and collectors. The governing story is the movement between figure and field: body, room, unit, serial structure, gravity-formed face, and open color system.
The escape from Soviet Armenia should enter through biography, chronology, and a restrained interpretive passage. It must never function as a cinematic homepage device or a shortcut to significance. The public argument is that the work transforms conditions of rupture into a formal language of portable units, repeatable actions, and structures that can be remade.

[Heading1] 5. Work navigation: curated path plus complete index
Do not reproduce the current seventeen-item grid as the primary experience. It treats major historical bodies and undeveloped image folders as equivalent. The primary Work page should be a curated retrospective sequence organized by the five chapters. A secondary All Works / Series Index should preserve every collection name, legacy URL, and object record.
INFORMATION-ARCHITECTURE RULE  Chronology explains evolution; themes explain continuity. The new site needs both. Use the chapter structure for the main narrative and chronological filters inside the complete index.
Every legacy collection URL should redirect to its new destination so existing links and search history are preserved.
A work can belong to more than one chapter. Foreigners can appear under Body / Emergence and in process filters; Waves can bridge Unit / Structure and Open Structure.
Do not publish all Studies at equal scale. Build edited sequences of 12-20 images with a path to the deeper archive.
Separate artworks from process photographs, installation views, studio documentation, and ephemera.

[Heading1] 8. Visual direction
Use an austere editorial system: warm white, black, calibrated gray, and one muted oxide accent. Let gold and color come from the artworks, not from decorative interface effects.
Pair one contemporary grotesk sans serif with one restrained reading serif at most. Typography should feel archival and current, not luxury-branded.
Favor generous margins, full-width installation views, and disciplined caption placement. Scale should be felt through bodies, doorways, rooms, and details.
Use motion only to support duration and sequence: slow fades, measured image changes, and optional process video. No glitter, parallax spectacle, simulated gold, floating particles, or cinematic escape reenactment.
Show chronology through visual recurrence: room to block, block to stack, stack to body, body to movable color. Avoid a single uninterrupted wall of gold images.
Design captions as primary scholarship, not footnotes. They must remain legible on mobile and visible without hovering.

[Heading1] 10. Canon-positioning protocol
The site should present Zadikian as a participant in the historical field before describing him as an omission from it. The public proof sequence is: early New York venue; total-space work; MoMA PS1 record; documented Serra collaboration; return of the figure; long development of serial gold structures; renewed institutional sequence; open color work in Venice. This establishes continuity and consequence.
Homepage: one restrained line about his place inside the experimental field of Minimalism and post-Minimalism.
Biography: dates, places, training, migration, key exhibitions, and collaborators; no extended theoretical comparison.
Work statement: the precise Minimalism/post-Minimalism argument.
Chronology: documentary evidence and links.
Commissioned essays: deeper comparisons to Cezanne, Newman, Rothko, Rauschenberg, Johns, Warhol, Christo, and other relevant figures.
PREFERRED PUBLIC CLAIM  Zadikian developed within the experimental New York field of Minimalism and post-Minimalism, then extended its serial and spatial vocabularies through gold, gravity, the human figure, and participatory structure.
[Heading1] 11. Content production plan
Lock the facts. Resolve birth year, migration chronology, early gold dates, Path to Nine count, and the complete exhibition record.
Inventory the archive. Assign a stable record to every artwork, installation view, process image, document, and text.
Edit the retrospective sequence. Select the 25-40 works that make the five-chapter argument before designing screens.
Commission missing photography. Prioritize early figurative works, room-scale pieces, stack variations, gravity-forming process, and the newest color works.
Clear rights. Record photographers, permissions, captions, and crop restrictions before image design begins.
Prototype the narrative. Test desktop and mobile versions of Home, Work chapter, Work detail, and Chronology before extending the system.
Run a curatorial QA. Check every canonical claim, venue role, date, collection reference, and acquisition implication.
Launch with redirects and an editorial calendar. Preserve all old URLs and plan regular updates around Venice, essays, loans, and new work.
